# Code of Conduct

The GHA Cache Auditor community is committed to a respectful, welcoming
environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing code, ideas, and people.
- Accept good-faith criticism and focus feedback on the work.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Keep project discussions relevant and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, hostile language, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional project space.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
other project communications. Project maintainers may remove or edit
contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation, depending on the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method on the
[project maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/0then0). If no private
contact method is available, ask maintainers for one without posting names or
incident details in a public issue. Do not share another person's private
information. Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect reporters'
privacy and explain enforcement decisions when doing so is safe.

This policy is adapted from the community standards used by open-source
projects, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
